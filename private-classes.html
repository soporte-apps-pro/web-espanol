Frequently asked questions

What is included in each class?+

Each class is a private 50-minute session. Activities can include conversation, grammar, vocabulary, culture, personalized materials, and correction notes.

How do booking and payment work?+

You may view availability without blocking a time. When you start payment, the selected time is held for 10 minutes. It is confirmed only after Elkin verifies your payment reference.

Can I cancel or reschedule?+

Yes. Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice. Late cancellations and missed classes are deducted from the remaining class balance.

Should I choose the Speaking Club instead?+

Choose the Speaking Club for a fixed six-week small-group conversation experience. Choose private coaching for individual goals, flexible private scheduling, or intensive personalized support.
