Before your first class

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to feel ready before booking your Spanish class.

Is this program right for my level? +

These classes are designed primarily for B1–B2 learners. If you already understand everyday Spanish but need more fluency, vocabulary and confidence when speaking, this program is for you.

What is included in each class? +

Each class is a private 45-minute session focused on your goals. Activities can include real conversation, grammar, vocabulary, culture, personalized materials, and correction notes.

How do booking and payment work? +

First, choose an available time through Calendly. You will receive the booking details, and payment will be confirmed with you personally before the class.

Will the available times match my time zone? +

Calendly displays the selected time zone during booking. Check that it matches your location before confirming your class.

Can I cancel or reschedule a class? +

Yes. Please provide at least 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations and missed classes are deducted from your remaining class balance.

Which option should I choose? +

Choose a single class to try the experience or work on one topic. The four-class pack supports steady monthly progress, while the eight-class pack is best for more intensive practice and is valid for two months.
