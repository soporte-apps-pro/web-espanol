Your six-session path

Every week has a practical conversation goal.

Topics may adapt to the group, while the speaking skills follow a clear progression.

  1. 01

    Speak without overthinking

    Introductions, follow-up questions, and strategies for keeping a conversation alive.

  2. 02

    Tell a clear story

    Organize past experiences with useful sequencing language and detail.

  3. 03

    Explain what you think

    State an opinion, support it with reasons, and invite another perspective.

  4. 04

    Agree and disagree naturally

    React politely, clarify misunderstandings, and handle different points of view.

  5. 05

    Discuss real-world content

    Respond to a short story, video, cultural topic, or everyday situation.

  6. 06

    Conversation challenge

    Put the skills together and receive focused feedback for your next step.

Frequently asked questions

Is this club right for my level?+

The first cohort is specifically for B1 learners: people who understand familiar topics and core grammar but still hesitate, translate mentally, or struggle to express ideas naturally. The interest form helps us check the fit.

Will the class time be the same every week?+

Yes. Before payment, compatible applicants will receive the proposed weekly day and time. Once the group is confirmed, that schedule remains fixed for all six sessions. Please enroll only if you can attend the same agreed time each week.

How much does the program cost?+

The introductory price is US$84 total for the complete program (future groups: US$114): six live sessions, one session per week, with each session lasting 60 minutes. Preparation materials are included. Payment is requested only after your group and schedule are confirmed.

What happens if I miss a session?+

This is one complete six-session package with fixed program dates. Once the program begins, a session you miss is not refundable, transferable to another person, accumulated as credit, or replaced individually. If Elkin cancels a session, that session will be rescheduled.

What if I withdraw after the program begins?+

Enrollment covers the complete six-week program rather than separate weekly purchases. If you choose to withdraw after the first session has begun, payments and unused sessions are not refundable or kept as credit, subject to applicable law.

Do I pay when I apply?+

No. Applying is free. If one of your selected groups is confirmed, you receive the exact six-week schedule and decide whether to enroll before making any payment.
