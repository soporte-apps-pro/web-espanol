Frequently asked questions

Is this club right for my level?+

The founding group is specifically for B1 learners: people who understand familiar topics and core grammar but still hesitate, translate mentally, or struggle to express ideas naturally. The interest form helps us check the fit.

When will the group begin?+

The schedule will be confirmed after we identify at least four compatible B1 learners. Before any payment is requested, selected applicants will receive the exact start date, end date, and fixed weekly day and time for all six sessions.

Will the class time be the same every week?+

Yes. Before payment, compatible applicants will receive the proposed weekly day and time. Once the group is confirmed, that schedule remains fixed for all six sessions. Please enroll only if you can attend the same agreed time each week.

How much does the program cost?+

The founding price is US$84 for the complete program: six live sessions in total, one session per week, with each session lasting 60 minutes. Preparation materials are included. Payment is requested only after your group and schedule are confirmed.

What happens if I miss a session?+

This is one complete six-session package with fixed program dates. Once the program begins, a session you miss is not refundable, transferable to another person, accumulated as credit, or replaced individually. If Elkin cancels a session, that session will be rescheduled.

What if I withdraw after the program begins?+

Enrollment covers the complete six-week program rather than separate weekly purchases. If you choose to withdraw after the first session has begun, payments and unused sessions are not refundable or kept as credit, subject to applicable law.

Do I pay when I join the priority list?+

No. Joining the list simply tells us about your level, time zone, availability, and interest. You decide whether to enroll only after receiving the confirmed details.
